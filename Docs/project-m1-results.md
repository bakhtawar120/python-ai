# Module 1 Project: AI Test Results

 **Business: Bakhtawar Couture, boutique, Lahore**
    **Model: llama3.2:1b**

 **Prompt 1: Marketing**

 **Prompt:** Write a short Instagram post for Bakhtawar Couture, a boutique in Lahore. Target women and college girls. Friendly tone, 1 call to action.
**AI answer:** A friendly Instagram post that greets “beauties”, says Bakhtawar Couture is the go-to place for the latest fashion in Lahore, mentions outfits for school and chic dresses, asks people to DM to book an appointment, and adds three hashtags (#BakhtawarCouture #LahoreFashion #WearYourStyle).
**Useful?** Mostly yes. The tone is friendly and there is one call to action. But “outfits for school” does not fit college girls, and “book your appointment” was made up because a boutique does not usually take appointments.

 **Prompt 2: Customer Service**

 **Prompt**: You are the manager of Bakhtawar Couture. Write a polite reply to a customer whose order arrived late. Apologise and offer a small discount.
**AI answer**: A polite formal letter that apologises for the delay, offers a discount on the next purchase, and ends with a thank you and a request to contact us with questions.
**Useful?** Mostly yes. The tone is polite and professional. But it says “10% discount” while the code is “DELAY15” (15%), and it invented a checkout code that the boutique does not have. It also leaves [Customer’s Name] and [Your Name] blank.

 **Prompt 3: Growth**

**Prompt**: Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a boutique in Lahore can use to attract women and college girls with a budget of 20000 PKR per month.
**AI answer:** Three tools: Google Analytics (free), Lead Grabber (500 PKR per month), and Facebook Ads Management (5,000 PKR per month), all within the budget.
****Useful?** Partly. The budget is respected, but “Lead Grabber” may be a made-up tool and I could not verify its price. Google Analytics is for websites, and a small boutique may only use Instagram. So the ideas need checking before use.
